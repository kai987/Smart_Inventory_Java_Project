## Changes to P005-P024

Each source image (original or Commons-provided resized preview) was auto-oriented,
proportionally scaled to fit a 720 × 540 pixel canvas, padded where needed with
`#f6f5f1`, flattened if transparent, and encoded as WebP at quality 82. No part of
the source image was cropped, stretched, retouched, or generatively modified.
Embedded metadata was removed. The full original source framing is preserved;
photographic framing in the original remains as supplied by its photographer.

The versions of P001-P004 retain their earlier crop/scale processing, documented
in the repository's `docs/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`.
