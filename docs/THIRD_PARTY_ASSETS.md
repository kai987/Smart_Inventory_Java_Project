### Processing and License Notes

All twenty expanded-catalog assets use the same processing: auto-orient, resize
proportionally to fit a 720 × 540 pixel frame, add `#f6f5f1` canvas padding where
needed, flatten transparency against that canvas, and encode lossy WebP at
quality 82. Metadata was removed. No source image was cropped or stretched;
there was no retouching, watermark removal, color adjustment, or generative
modification. This preserves the original photographer's framing. P001-P004
were not reprocessed or replaced during this expansion.

The basketball creator's required credit is **Photography by User: MrX**, retained
here and in the distributed credits file. For P018 the source description also
contains an informal public-domain dedication; the project conservatively
retains the Commons-listed CC BY 2.0 license and creator credit. P024's creator
attribution is explicitly described by Commons as assumed from copyright claims,
not as independently verified authorship. P007 uses its author's worldwide
public-domain dedication; P009 and P024 use their copyright holders' PD-self
statements.

For each CC BY-SA asset, the resized/padded WebP version is made available under
the same CC BY-SA license linked in its row. CC BY assets retain their respective
CC BY terms. P005, P021, and P023 use the custom Pexels License, **not** CC0; the
Pexels license permits website/app and e-commerce illustration and modification
without mandatory attribution, while prohibiting unaltered-photo sales and
implied endorsements. Creator/source records are retained anyway.
